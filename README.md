## Ghostly
Yeah I'm ghostly ill


Upon death you will be turned into a ghost.
As a ghost you,
- Cannot interact with entities
- Mine blocks slower
- Your inventory is limited to just your hands
- Cannot craft inside of your inventory
- Become trans-parent

You can only be revived by an admin
